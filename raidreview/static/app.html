How each death is judged

Wipe deaths: once five players die within three seconds, or after the fifth death of a pull, the rest are part of the wipe. They are dimmed and left out of the totals unless you include them. Each pull names its wipe trigger.

No damage logged: no killing blow was logged and the player was above 30% health in their last two seconds. That is usually a knock off the platform, a fall or a scripted instant kill, so defensives and heals are not judged.

Avoidable: the killing blow is an ability ticked under "What killed people". Some bosses start with a list from public guides, the rest start with nothing ticked. Tick what your strat says is avoidable; the list is saved per boss.

Defensive ready: the player had a personal, Healthstone or health potion off cooldown, and the overkill was under 45% of their max health. A player only gets checked for a defensive they cast at least once in this review, so untalented spells never count. Cooldowns are base values, shortened when the player cast it faster than that in this review.

No heals: healing plus shield absorbs in the last 5 seconds came to under 10% of max health. The recap shows how many healers were alive; range is not in the log.

Hover or tab to any underlined ability to see what it does. Descriptions come from Wowhead.

Connect to Warcraft Logs

Raid Review reads logs through the Warcraft Logs API. Each person uses their own free API client.

  1. Open warcraftlogs.com/api/clients and sign in.
  2. Click Create Client. Use any name, enter http://localhost as the redirect URL, and leave "Public Client" unticked.
  3. Copy the client ID and the client secret into the boxes below.

Your client is stored only on this PC, in %APPDATA%\RaidReview.
